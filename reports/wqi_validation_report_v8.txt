IMPORTANT METHODOLOGICAL NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
This output is a WQI screening dataset, not a final WQI dataset.
The Min/Max average is used as a representative value where one or both measurements are available.
Broad plausibility limits are used only to flag values for review. No measurements are deleted because of these flags.
Eligibility means at least four of six selected parameters are available. It does not establish that a valid WQI can be calculated.
Nitrate measurements from different source definitions must be reconciled before final WQI calculation.
Final WQI calculation requires verified units, an appropriate river-water quality standard, parameter-specific ideal values, quality ratings and justified weights.
